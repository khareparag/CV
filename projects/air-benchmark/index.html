Live Instrument

Global Air Freight Contract Benchmark

This is a working pricing instrument. Twenty observed air freight lanes, each with one contract benchmark in USD per kg. Six corridor indices with the award month each one implies. Directional premiums between paired corridors. Live feeds for jet fuel, crude and public reference indices, each figure with its as-of date.

Two audiences, one engine. A freight buyer places a quoted rate against the lane benchmark and reads whether the quote holds up. A forwarder reads the same maths from the selling side. Rates convert from any of 30 currencies at the published reference rate, and the conversion is always shown.

The frame below is the live product at air.rfq.ch. It updates itself. This page always shows the current version. The full methodology, from lane capture to benchmark derivation, comes in person: pk@rfq.ch.
